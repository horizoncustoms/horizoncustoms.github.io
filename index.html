About OceanWorks

OceanWorks is committed to building modern, reliable, and powerful Discord solutions. Our focus is creating tools that help server owners moderate, protect, and manage their communities with ease.

Projects

🤖 Ocean One

Ocean One is a powerful all-in-one Discord bot built for ERLC communities, featuring moderation, logging, verification, raid protection, custom commands, and advanced server management tools. Designed to help server owners streamline operations and enhance member experiences, Ocean One provides everything needed to keep communities secure, organized, and thriving.
